Mission, Vision
& Values

At Sunset Dentistry, our values guide every interaction, shaping how we care for our patients and contribute to our community.

Mission

To provide patient-focused healthcare powered by compassion, expertise, and accessibility for everyone. We don't just treat the mouth. We treat the individual, ensuring every patient feels valued and cared for.

Vision

To be a leading healthcare provider, empowering our community through exceptional preventive dentistry that improves overall health, where every patient achieves optimal oral health through compassionate, innovative care.

Values

We believe in putting our patients first, working with compassion, and constantly pushing the boundaries of what's possible, for our patients, our partners, and our community.

Meet our dentists

Our leading dentists bring decades of experience and unwavering commitment to excellence.

Dr. Marco Leyte-VidalDMD

Dr. Marco Leyte-Vidal was born in Havana, Cuba, and grew up in Miami, Florida, where his Cuban roots have always stayed close to home. He went on to study at both the University of Miami and the University of Florida, laying the academic foundation for the career in dentistry that would follow.

Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Florida College of Dentistry in 1986.

Dr. Leyte-Vidal is an active member of the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, South Florida District Dental Association, and International Team for Implantology.

Spent five years as an assistant professor in the Division of Oral and Maxillofacial Dentistry at the University of Miami School of Medicine and is a former two-term President of the Riviera Dental Study Club and a Fellow of the Academy of Sports Dentistry.

Dr. Michael RobinsonDMD

Dr. Michael Robinson grew up in Coventry, Rhode Island, where a love of science took root early. In 1987, he traded New England winters for Miami sunshine to attend the University of Miami, earning his Bachelor of Science in Biology and falling for the city he'd go on to call home.

Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine in 1995.

Dr. Robinson is an active member of the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, South Florida District Dental Association, International Team for Implantology, and Academy for Sports Dentistry.

Dr. Shelley RobinsonDMD

Dr. Shelley Robinson was born and raised in Miami, Florida, and today cares for patients in the same community she grew up in. She earned her bachelor's degree at the University of Florida and stayed on for dental school, building the foundation for a career centered on the people she treats.

Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Florida College of Dentistry.

Dr. Shelley Robinson is an active member of the American Dental Association and Florida Dental Association.

Joined Sunset Dentistry in 2023 and takes a conservative, biologically informed, whole-person approach to care, preserving natural tooth structure and tailoring treatment to each patient's concerns, goals, and overall health so every patient feels heard, comfortable, and confident in their care.
